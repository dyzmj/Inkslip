# Built-in writing presets

These stylesheets are independently written for Inkslip. They use system fonts and contain no bundled font files, imported stylesheets, or external images. Preset filenames are retained as configuration identifiers for existing installations; these files are Inkslip presets, not distributions of other theme packages.

User-installed themes and their licenses are managed separately by the user. Third-party dependencies retain their notices in the installed dependency packages.
